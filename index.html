About this project

The LPU Library Seat and Resource Booking System imagines how students at Lovely Professional University could reserve study spaces and library resources online. It was created to practise semantic HTML5 structure, responsive CSS3 layouts, forms and accessible design.

Library services

Sample service categories used to demonstrate the interface.

01

Seat Booking

02

04

Reference Books

Request course and reference material for your visit.

How it works

  1. Choose

    Pick your date, time and study zone.

  2. Select

    Tap a seat on the sample layout map.

  3. Confirm

    Review the booking summary on screen.
